Before you start

  • You don't need the second panel. This panel runs a pretend UPSTAIRS panel inside its own firmware. From the Test screen it rings you, answers your rings after 3 seconds, and can be unplugged. The pretend panel makes no sound, so every sound you hear is this panel following the rules.
  • The clock is set (from the laptop, at 7:51 AM). Parts A to C run in daytime; part D needs night time.
  • In Settings, Volume should read 4 of 5 (that's 90) and Ringtone Westminster.
  • This panel is set to DOWNSTAIRS, so the other floor is upstairs and banners read "Doorbell from upstairs".
  • To make the other floor ring you: tap the gear, scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap TEST PANEL (the last tile, bottom right). Then tap Other floor rings this panel (third row, left). The Test screen stays open and the banner appears on top of it.
  • To change your own status: tap your DOWNSTAIRS tile and pick one. Private and Sleeping open the timer screen: choose Until changed, then Set status.
  • On the Test screen, Other floor answers should say Come on.
